The workroom

Nothing is made until it's ordered.

Solenne Vane keeps no stockroom. A dress is cut only once it's been asked for — pattern, fitting, and finish, in that order, by the same two hands each time. Bronze thread pulled tight by eye, hems felled by hand.

Leather is aged in small batches, velvet is cut against the grain on purpose — the kind of detail that only shows once you're wearing it.
